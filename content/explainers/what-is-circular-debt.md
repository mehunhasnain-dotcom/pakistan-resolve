## The basic idea

Think of the power sector as a chain. Consumers pay distribution companies. Distribution companies pay for the electricity they buy. Buyers pay power producers. Producers pay for fuel. When money does not move along the chain fully, each link is left owing the next, and the shortfall accumulates. The result is often called [[circular-debt]].

::: fact
This describes the general concept only. It is a simplified picture, not a statement about how large the amount is or what causes it in Pakistan.
:::

## Why the definition matters

Two sources can both say "circular debt" and mean different things, for example a stock owed at a point in time versus a flow added over a year. A finished version of this page should record which definition each source uses.

## What is debated

Different groups explain the buildup differently, for example by pointing to prices, contracts, losses, collections or subsidies. Each explanation needs an attributed source and an evidence check.
