## What the fuel system does

Fuel is moved from where it is produced or imported to millions of end users. Along the way it passes through several hands, and each adds costs, taxes or margins. The pump price is the sum of that chain.

The stages in "The system at a glance" are general. The research questions for Pakistan are: how much fuel is imported versus refined here, how it is stored and moved, who sells it, and what stops or slows supply when something goes wrong.

## What decides the price

The pump price is shaped by several things. Candidates to research are the international price of the product, the exchange rate ([[exchange-rate]]), taxes and each [[levy]], and margins for companies along the chain. Which ones apply, how they are combined and how often the price is reviewed are all open research questions on this page.
