## Content status labels

Every system, explainer and glossary term carries a status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | A structure or working text. Not checked against sources. Do not cite. |
| **Reviewed** | Claims have been checked against the cited sources by someone other than the author. |
| **Published** | Reviewed, and considered ready as a reference. |

The build refuses to mark a page Reviewed or Published unless it lists at least one cited source.

## Four kinds of statement

Where the difference matters, a statement is labelled in a box that names its kind, so meaning never depends on colour alone.

::: fact
**Fact:** supported by identifiable evidence, with a source.
:::

::: official
**Official position:** what an institution or person says, which is not the same as being correct.
:::

::: analysis
**Analysis:** an interpretation of evidence and incentives. Reasonable people may disagree.
:::

::: proposal
**Proposal:** a possible action, with its likely trade-offs and limits.
:::

::: todo
**Research needed:** a gap on a draft page that must be filled and sourced before review.
:::

## Sources

Where practical, sources are preferred in this order:

1. Primary sources: laws, notifications, regulator documents, official datasets, budget documents, parliamentary records.
2. Research institutions, academic work and established international organisations.
3. Credible journalism, for reporting and context.
4. Expert commentary, treated as interpretation.

An official source is not automatically complete or neutral, especially where an institution has its own interests.

Source lists separate **Cited sources** (claims on the page rest on them) from **Where to look**, which are starting points for research that have not yet been checked. A site listed under *Where to look* has not been used to verify anything.

## Figures

A figure is shown only with its value, unit, period covered and source. If any of these is missing, the build fails. Old figures are not presented as current: the date the data describes is kept separate from the date the page was written.

## Uncertainty and disagreement

Where data is missing, contested or defined differently by different sources, the page says so.

## Corrections and updates

Updates change the page's updated date. A consequential factual correction is recorded on the page rather than silently rewritten. To report an error, see [Corrections and contact](/corrections/).

## Independence

Claims, institutions and administrations are judged by the same evidence standards regardless of party or personality. Any future funding arrangement must preserve editorial independence and be disclosed.

## Use of AI

AI tools may help organise, draft or transform research. They are not a source. Every factual claim and citation must be checked against the real source before a page is marked Reviewed.
