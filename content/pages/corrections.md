## What to send

- **An error:** the page, the sentence, and what is wrong.
- **A source:** a link or document that supports, contradicts or improves a claim.
- **A topic:** a system or question you would like explained.

## What happens next

Corrections are checked against the source. If a consequential claim changes, the page's updated date changes and a correction note is added rather than the text being changed silently.
