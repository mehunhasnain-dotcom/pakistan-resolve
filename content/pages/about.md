## Why this project exists

Information about Pakistan is spread across news reports, government websites, regulator documents, research papers and social media. It is hard for an ordinary reader to connect those pieces. A headline can report a price change without explaining who decided it, what the process was, or where the money goes.

Pakistan Resolve is an independent project that tries to close that gap. It explains the systems behind the headlines: institutions, energy, the economy, public finance and public services.

## From a news idea to a knowledge hub

The project began as a broad idea for a news and awareness agency: a lighter alternative to television news. That origin still shapes its tone, which is accessible and civic-minded. But the lasting value is in structured explanations, not in racing newsrooms on speed. So the organising principle is **systems first, news second**: a current event is an entry point into a durable explanation.

## What you can expect

- **Short explainers** that answer one question, linked to the wider system.
- **System pages** that map how part of public life works, step by step.
- **Visible sources and dates**, and clear labels for what is established, what is an official position, what is analysis and what is a proposal.
- **Plain language** that respects your intelligence without assuming specialist knowledge.

## What the project does not claim

It does not claim to solve Pakistan's problems, to be free of all bias, or to cover everything. It is not a breaking-news service, a campaign, or a government portal. A website cannot fix national problems. It can make systems easier to understand, question and discuss.

## Where things stand

This is an early build. Every page is currently labelled **Draft**: the structure and the research questions are in place, but the content has not yet been checked against sources. Pages move to **Reviewed** only when they cite sources. See [Editorial standards](/standards/).
