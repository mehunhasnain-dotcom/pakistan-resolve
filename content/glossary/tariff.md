---
title: Tariff
summary: The approved schedule of prices that a utility is allowed to charge for a service.
status: draft
updated: 2026-10-15
related_systems: [electricity]
draft_note: General definition only. How tariffs are approved and structured in Pakistan has not been verified.

In general, a tariff sets out the rates a provider may charge, often with different rates for different kinds of customer or levels of use. An approved tariff is not always the same as what customers end up paying once adjustments, taxes and fees are added.
