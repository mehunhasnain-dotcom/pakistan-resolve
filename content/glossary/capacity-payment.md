---
title: Capacity payment
summary: A payment to a power plant for being available to generate, separate from payment for the electricity it actually produces.
status: draft
updated: 2026-10-15
related_systems: [electricity]
draft_note: General definition only. How capacity payments are set and paid in Pakistan has not been verified.
---

The idea is to reward availability as well as output, so that plants can cover fixed costs even when they run less. How such payments work in practice depends on the specific contracts, which need to be checked against primary documents.
